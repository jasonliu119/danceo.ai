# AI Dog Dance Video Ideas

An AI dog dance video pairs a dog photo or original dog character with a dance reference. A clear subject and a readable movement give you a useful starting point for a playful clip.

## Three concepts to try

1. **Small rhythmic gestures:** pair a centered dog portrait or character with a short reference featuring simple upper-body movement.
2. **Full-body performance:** use an image with the character's body visible and a reference with steady, full-body framing.
3. **Character introduction:** choose a recognizable original dog character and a brief movement that matches its personality.

These are creative starting points, not guarantees of a particular result. Compare a few references to see which motion fits your image.

## Before generating

Check that the dog is prominent, well lit, and not obscured. Use an image and a reference video you own or are authorized to transform. Review the selected inputs, output duration, and displayed credit cost before creating the video.

For more detail, read [Danceo's AI dog dance guide](https://danceo.ai/ai-dog-dance) and the [input checklist](photo-and-reference.md).
